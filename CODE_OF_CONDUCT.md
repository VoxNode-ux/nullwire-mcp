# Code of Conduct

NullWire-MCP is a small, volunteer-run project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests or discussions, is expected to keep it a respectful place to work.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the code, not the person.
- Give clear, specific feedback, and accept it the same way.
- Respect that people have different experience, time and first languages.
- Keep reports and examples free of real secrets: use fake tokens, cookies and addresses.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or trolling
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors for faster answers

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, comments) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. They decide in good faith and are not obliged to debate each decision.

To report a conduct problem, use the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** and start the message with "Conduct report", or contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile [@VoxNode-ux](https://github.com/VoxNode-ux). Reports are handled privately.
